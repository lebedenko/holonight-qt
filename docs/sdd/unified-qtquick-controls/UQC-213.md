## Shared contract (settled before implementation)

Holonight.Core exports style-independent attached `HnInputInteraction` on Items
and Windows. `hoverAllowed` is read-only; `suppressHover()` gives keyboard authority
to newly opened surfaces; `pointerMoved(point scenePosition)` reports changed
screen coordinates in the attached window's scene coordinates. Shared state is
owned per QQuickWindow. Non-modifier key presses suppress hover. Real MouseMove
with changed screen coordinates restores hover and emits movement even within an
already-hovered item. Enter/hover events, layout/animation/scroll, pointer presses
and wheel alone do not authorize hover selection. Input is observed, never consumed.
Clicks retain immediate activation. Different windows are independent; detached
items do not authorize hover, reattachment follows their new window safely.

Apply gating only to HoloNight-owned hover rendering in standard style controls
and composites, including selectable/icon delegates. Preserve selected, checked,
pressed and keyboard-focus rendering. Do not modify Fusion standard-control QML.
